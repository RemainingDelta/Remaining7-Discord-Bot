# Remaining 7 Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Remaining 7 Bot collects when you use it in the Remaining 7 Discord server, how that information is used, and how it is protected. This policy applies only to the bot's own data practices and is separate from Discord's own Privacy Policy, which governs the Discord platform itself.

## Who we are

Remaining 7 Bot is operated by Remaining 7, a Brawl Stars esports organization, for use within its own Discord server. This is a private bot built for a single community and is not available for other servers to add.

## What information we collect

When you interact with the bot, we may collect and store the following:

- Your Discord user ID, used to identify your account across all bot features
- Token and XP balances, and level, tracked as part of the server's economy system
- Quest progress, including which quests are assigned to you and your completion status
- Redemption and ticket history, including items purchased, tickets opened, and which staff member fulfilled a request
- Account-security flags, if your account is ever marked as compromised through the bot's "hacked" protocol (this record contains your user ID and a fixed reason label, not the content of any flagged messages)
- Sticky message text, if a staff member sets a sticky message in a channel using the bot
- Booster status markers, a simple monthly marker used to track whether you've used a booster perk (such as a shop discount) in the current month

## What we do not collect or store

- We do not store your Discord roles, nickname, or other full profile information
- We do not store your raw Discord boost start date; we only store a monthly marker used for perk eligibility
- We do not use Discord's Presence intent, so we never see your online status or activity
- We do not store the content of your everyday messages. Message content is read live to power features like commands, quests, and moderation, but it is not saved, with the single exception of sticky message text described above

## Why we collect this information

All information collected is necessary to operate the bot's features, including:

- The token and XP economy, leveling, and daily rewards
- Quest assignment and tracking
- The shop, redemption, and ticket systems
- Tournament ticket management
- The account-security ("hacked") protocol, used to protect compromised accounts
- Sticky messages and other staff moderation tools

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating these features.

## Where your information is stored

All data is stored in our own MongoDB Atlas database. It is encrypted at rest and encrypted in transit between the bot and the database. We do not share, sell, or license this information to any third party, data broker, or advertising service.

## When information leaves Discord

In a small number of cases, information generated by the bot is sent outside of Discord:

- Ticket transcripts. When a support or tournament ticket is closed, the bot generates a text file containing that ticket's message history. This file is sent to the person who opened the ticket via direct message and is archived in a staff-only log channel.
- GitHub issue creation. A single authorized staff member can @mention the bot to convert a bug report or feature request into a GitHub issue. When this happens, that staff member's message is sent to Google's Gemini API for classification and to GitHub, where the resulting issue is created and publicly visible in our project repository. This feature is restricted to one authorized user and is not available to general members.

## Opt-out and your choices

There is currently no opt-out mechanism for passive data collection, such as token/XP earning or quest tracking, while you remain a member of the server. Leaving the server stops any further collection going forward but does not automatically delete existing records.

If you would like your data deleted, you can contact us using the information below and we will remove your stored records upon request, except where retention is required to preserve the integrity of shared records (such as a ticket transcript already shared with another user).

## Age requirement

This bot and the server it operates in are not directed at children. In line with Discord's own Terms of Service, you must be at least 13 years old, or the minimum age required in your country, to use Discord and this bot.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the bot's features change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request that your data be deleted, you can reach us by opening a ticket in the tickets channel (Server Support category) in the Remaining 7 Discord server.
